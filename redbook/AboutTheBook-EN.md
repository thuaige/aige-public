# The AIGE Red Book

## *Artificial Intelligence General Education: Cognitive Autonomy in an Intelligent Society*

*Artificial Intelligence General Education: Cognitive Autonomy in an Intelligent Society* is authored by Dong Wang and Shaoping Ma and published by the Center for Artificial Intelligence General Education (AIGE), Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University. The book systematically examines the social context, theoretical foundations, educational goals, content framework and stage-specific design of Artificial Intelligence General Education, providing both a conceptual framework and a practical foundation for its development in an intelligent society.

## The Practical Background of AIGE

Artificial intelligence is increasingly involved in fundamental social activities, including knowledge production, information dissemination, judgment formation and social action. The ways in which people understand the world, form beliefs, allocate trust and make decisions are increasingly shaped by data, algorithms, models, platforms and artificial intelligence systems. AI education has therefore acquired a new general-education responsibility: helping individuals understand artificial intelligence and its social integration while maintaining human agency and sound judgment in complex cognitive environments.

The Tsinghua Artificial Intelligence General Education system, or AIGE, was developed in response to this transformation. It is a systematic educational initiative jointly developed by Tsinghua University’s Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University Press, Tsinghua University High School, Tsinghua University Primary School and collaborating partners. The system connects K–12 education, higher education, teacher education and lifelong learning.

AIGE has gradually established an integrated resource system encompassing textbooks, courses, teaching slides, student guides, teacher education and classroom pilots. Related resources have been presented and used through platforms including the National Smart Education Platform for Primary and Secondary Schools and the Beijing Smart Education Platform for Primary and Secondary Schools. Teaching practices have also been implemented in more than 300 pilot schools, supported by a network of over 200 certified instructors. These activities provide a practical foundation for the Red Book’s concepts, content structure and implementation pathways.

## Purpose of the Red Book

The Red Book addresses three fundamental questions concerning the development of Artificial Intelligence General Education:

1. Why does an intelligent society require Artificial Intelligence General Education?
2. Why can Artificial Intelligence General Education undertake the cognitive-development responsibilities of modern general education?
3. How can a coherent content framework, curriculum structure and assessment system be developed across different stages of education?

Beginning with the modern responsibilities of general education, the book places **cognitive autonomy** at the centre of its theoretical framework. Cognitive autonomy refers to the capacity of individuals to actively regulate their own belief formation, use of evidence, allocation of trust, boundaries of judgment and responsibility for action in complex cognitive environments.

Artificial Intelligence General Education is defined as an integration of general education and artificial intelligence education. It takes the concepts, knowledge, methods, applications and social consequences of artificial intelligence as its educational content. Through studying the scientific processes and social mechanisms embodied in artificial intelligence, learners develop four interconnected capabilities: induction, generalization, judgment and self-awareness. These capabilities help individuals understand an intelligent society, use artificial intelligence responsibly and remain accountable for their own cognition and actions.

The Red Book also seeks to establish a shared basis for discussion among AI researchers, education researchers, school leaders, teachers, curriculum developers, policymakers and nonprofit educational organizations. This shared foundation enables participants from different fields to collaborate on the goals, boundaries and implementation of Artificial Intelligence General Education.

## Contents of the Red Book

The book follows a conceptual progression connecting **general education, intelligent society, cognitive autonomy and Artificial Intelligence General Education**. It consists of four main parts.

### Part I: The Social Foundations and Conceptual Definition of Artificial Intelligence General Education

This part examines the modern responsibilities of general education in an intelligent society, analyzes how intelligent systems are transforming the human cognitive environment, introduces the concept of cognitive autonomy, and defines the nature, goals and boundaries of Artificial Intelligence General Education.

### Part II: The Cognitive Foundations of Artificial Intelligence General Education

Beginning with the scientific character of artificial intelligence, this part examines how intelligent activities are transformed into problems that can be represented, computed, tested and addressed through constructive processes. It then analyzes how the integration of artificial intelligence into society leads to the computational transformation of social activities, goals and relations. On this basis, the book explains how artificial intelligence and its socialization can support the development of induction, generalization, judgment and self-awareness.

### Part III: The Content Framework of Artificial Intelligence General Education

This part proposes a systematic content framework encompassing foundational AI concepts, core methods, representative applications, interdisciplinary fields, ethics and social responsibility. These areas help learners develop a coherent understanding of artificial intelligence while providing the learning contexts needed for cognitive development and transfer to social situations.

### Part IV: Educational Design Across Different Stages

This part examines the goals, content priorities and implementation approaches of Artificial Intelligence General Education in primary school, middle school, high school, higher education, vocational education, public education and lifelong learning. These stages follow a continuous developmental direction while adapting to learners’ cognitive characteristics, prior knowledge and evolving social roles.

## Open Editorial Process and Feedback

In response to the continuing rapid development of artificial intelligence and Artificial Intelligence General Education, the Red Book follows an open editorial approach. AI specialists, education experts, social science researchers and frontline teachers are invited to contribute through:

1. comments, corrections and suggestions concerning the existing content;
2. proposals and discussions concerning additional topics;
3. relevant theoretical studies, curriculum practices and teaching cases.

The Red Book will be updated in response to expert feedback and developments in educational practice. Individual contributions will be recognized in the “AIGE Contributors” section of the book.

Feedback and contributions may be submitted through:

1. GitHub Discussions: https://github.com/thuaige/aige-public/discussions
