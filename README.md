
## About This Repository

1. This repository is maintained for sharing resources developed by the Center for Artificial Intelligence General Education (AIGE), Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University. Schools and universities are welcome to apply to become AIGE teaching-research partners or pilot institutions and to contribute to the joint development of open educational resources.

2. Official AIGE publications and teaching resources are released through Zenodo (https://zenodo.org/communities/aige). GitHub is used for documentation, discussion, feedback and issue tracking. 

3. This repository is not governed by a single license. The permitted use of each resource is determined by the copyright and license notice accompanying that resource.


Official webpage: http://thuaige.org
Community webpage in Zenodo：https://zenodo.org/communities/aige
Contact: [aigraph@cslt.org](mailto:aigraph@cslt.org)

## 关于本资料库

1. 本资料库专门用于发布清华大学计算机系人工智能通识教育研究中心（AIGE）的共享资料。欢迎有关学校申请成为清华大学大中小学人工智能通识教育教学研究单位或教学试点单位，共同建设开放教育资源。
